Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information MaheHub (“we”, “us”) collects when you use the MaheHub app and the website smartdns.mahehub.com, and what we do with it. By using the app or website you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and your WhatsApp or phone number (including country code). Resellers also give an email address and a password.
  • Subscription details: your plan, start and expiry dates, and your account status.
  • Network address (IP address): the public IP address of the connection you activate. We use it to switch your Smart DNS service on for that connection.
  • Device identifier: in the mobile app, an identifier generated by your phone. It locks one account to one phone so a shared login cannot be passed around. It is not your name, phone number or advertising ID.
  • Security logs: login attempts and the IP address they came from, to stop guessing and abuse.
  • Messages you send us: when you contact support on WhatsApp or by email, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera data. The app does not read the content of your messages, calls or banking apps.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account and your subscription.
  • To switch the service on for your connection and keep one account on one phone.
  • To contact you about your account, renewals and support.
  • To protect the service from fraud and abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads or share your information with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with our database and authentication provider, Supabase, over encrypted connections. Access is limited to MaheHub staff who need it to run the service. Your seller (the reseller who set up your account) can see your account’s name or number, plan and expiry so they can support and renew you.

Payments

The app does not take card details. Payments are made to us or your seller by mobile wallet (for example bKash or Nagad) outside the app.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. After you ask us to delete your account (see below), we delete or anonymise it within 30 days, except records we must keep for security or legal reasons.

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data: see how to delete your account.
  • Remove the DNS setting or uninstall the app at any time.

Children

The service is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use after a change means you accept it.
